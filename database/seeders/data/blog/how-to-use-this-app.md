This app is built for learning a little Luganda every day, on your phone, even without a signal. Here is how to get the most out of it.

## Follow the path

Open [Learn](/learn) and you will see the course path: seven stages, from home and family to the market and the town. Each lesson takes a few minutes. Mukasa and Nakato teach you: they show a picture, say the word, and ask you to try. Tap 🔊 to listen again, and 🐢 to hear it slowly.

Got something wrong? No problem. Missed questions come back at the end of the lesson, so you finish with them right.

## Review a little every day

The words you learn come back in [Review](/review) just before you would forget them. Five minutes of review a day does more than an hour once a week. Your daily goal ring on the course path shows how you are doing.

## Tap any word

See a Luganda word you don't know? Tap it. You get its meaning, its recording and a link to the [dictionary](/dictionary). If a word is not in the dictionary yet, the app says so honestly instead of guessing.

## Play, and earn cowries

Long ago, cowrie shells were money in Buganda. In this app you earn them for lessons, reviews and [games](/games): Memory match, the talking drum, Lightning round, Spell it, Sentence bridge and Market run. Spend them in the wardrobe to dress Mukasa and Nakato in a new kanzu or gomesi.

## Say it out loud

On every new phrase you can record yourself and compare your voice with the recording. Nobody grades you: you decide whether it sounded the same. Your recordings are never kept.

## Take it with you

Add the app to your phone's home screen, and tap **Save for offline** on a stage before a journey. Lessons you have opened keep working without a signal, and your progress is sent when you are back online.

## A plan for your first month

Not sure where to start? The [four-week plan](/culture/getting-started) gives you a few lessons each week, plus a game and something from Ganda culture to explore.
